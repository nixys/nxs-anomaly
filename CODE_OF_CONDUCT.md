# Community Code of Conduct

We want nxs-anomaly to be a welcoming place to ask questions, share experience
and improve the project. These rules apply to everyone, including maintainers,
in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed community
spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate of different backgrounds, identities, experience
  levels and ways of communicating. Questions from newcomers are welcome.
- Discuss ideas and code on their merits. Give specific, constructive feedback;
  disagreement is welcome without personal attacks.
- Assume that context may be missing. Ask for clarification and acknowledge
  mistakes or corrections.
- Keep conversations relevant, respect other people's time and accept reasonable
  requests from moderators to stop or move a discussion.
- Protect privacy. Share logs and examples only after removing credentials and
  personal or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, slurs, sexualized comments or unwanted sexual attention.
- Threats, intimidation, personal insults or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing someone else's private information or private correspondence without
  permission, or threatening to do so.
- Spam, repeated unwanted contact or retaliation against someone who reports a
  concern or participates in its review.

## Reporting a concern

Contact **[p.rukin@nixys.io](mailto:p.rukin@nixys.io)** privately. Include links to
relevant messages, a description of what happened and any context you are
comfortable sharing. You may report behavior you experienced or witnessed.
Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

If your concern involves that contact, or you prefer another channel, use the
[Nixys contact page](https://nixys.io/contacts/) and ask for a different person to
review a community conduct concern. A person directly involved in a complaint
should not decide its outcome.

Reports should be handled discreetly. Information should be shared only with
people needed to review and address the concern; absolute confidentiality cannot
be guaranteed. We may ask for clarification and will communicate the outcome to
the reporter where possible without exposing another person's private information.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate reporting process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Product bugs and installation questions belong in
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/nixys/nxs-anomaly/issues).

## Moderation and review

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock a discussion,
issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation in
project-managed spaces. The response should reflect the severity, impact and
whether the behavior is repeated. Serious threats or harassment may require
immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

Where practical, moderators should explain which rule was violated and what must
change. To request a review of a moderation decision, use the private contacts
above and provide the relevant context. Someone not directly involved should
review the request where possible.
